About

Profile

In a world where content is king, I am the kingmaker. For me the experience of storytelling deserves to reach people, where they are, on time and at scale, from the biggest live events to the smallest FAST channel.

I started in programme planning at Discovery and NBCUniversal, trained as a director at the London MET Film School, and moved into product. Today I own streaming infrastructure, metadata and vendor partnerships, working side by side with engineers and using data to decide what to build next.
